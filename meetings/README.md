# Meeting Notes

All CMPE 295A/295B project meetings for the **Autonomous AI-Agent Marketplace** are recorded here, one file per meeting.

## Naming convention
- `YYYY-MM-DD-advisor.md`: meetings with all team members and the project advisor (Prof. Rakesh Ranjan)
- `YYYY-MM-DD-team.md`: team-only meetings

Every note follows the course Meeting Notes Template (copy [`TEMPLATE.md`](TEMPLATE.md)): meeting details and
attendees, accomplished since last meeting, accomplished during this meeting, issues/blockers, action items
(action | owner | due) and plans for the next meeting. Open action items are carried forward and closed in later notes.

## Meeting cadence
| Type | Frequency | Slot |
|---|---|---|
| Team-only sync | Weekly | Sunday 02:30PM to 03:00PM |
| Advisor meeting | Every two weeks | Mondays 8:30PM to 09:00PM |
